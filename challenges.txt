[demo page] CSS display rules beat the hidden attribute, so the loading line never went away > one global [hidden]{display:none} rule
[AI v1] On "Not the Christmas heist", the AI flagged "Not the" (a slang pattern) instead of "Christmas heist". Both are real gaps, but the hinge of the joke was missed > decide in the prompt and test set which one is the hinge
[AI v1] First live read took about 15 seconds > keep the loading line honest, add "Still reading..." after 8 seconds
[extension] "What I read" showed Instagram labels ("오리지널 오디오", "1주 · 수정됨") and skipped the caption and most visible comments > read only the sentence text (skip links, time, buttons), keep the caption, pick the area with the most text
[AI v1] On a long parody comment, the AI flagged "the doctor said it was a false alarm" and missed the real hinge, "Hallmark" > prompt v2 adds general rules: brand/channel/show names can be hinges, punchlines sit at the end of long comments, the first item may be the caption (no test answers named in the prompt)
[AI speed] A post took about 12 seconds at medium effort. Low effort was 6 seconds but missed the main joke ("Christmas heist"). Fast mode was not available on this account > kept medium, cut perceived wait instead (cache per post, 0.5s between frames)
[extension] The panel floated over Instagram and hid the comments on the right side of the reel > shrink the page by the panel width while the panel is open, so the reel and comments slide left (400px still clipped the edge of Instagram's comment box, so I added a 100px cushion)
[extension] New comments from scrolling showed up so late (1.2s wait + about 10s AI) that I almost missed them > scroll wait 1.2s to 0.5s, and a "Reading new comments…" sticker shows the moment a read starts. Kept medium effort (D24: measure before trading accuracy)
[extension] The "Reading new comments…" sticker was not enough: it vanished in 1.6s, long before the AI answered, and cards appeared silently below the fold > a pill at the bottom of the panel that stays while reading, then says "N new cards below" (or "Nothing new to decode."); new cards glow lime once. Read new comments in up to 3 parallel batches (first batch shows first), scroll wait 0.5s to 0.3s, rate limit 8 to 20 calls a minute
[extension] Selecting words to ask about is invisible: nothing tells a new user they can drag > three quiet hints in the product: a mono line under the cards, a one-time sticker after the first read, and the lime "Ask IYKYK" sticker itself that appears right next to the selection
[extension] When the comments are closed the panel only said "Open the comments" in text, and a new user doesn't know where to look > the same moment, a pulsing lime ring + "Open comments →" sticker points at Instagram's comment icon on the page
[extension] Even with a 100px cushion, the comment box touched the panel: I was clipping Instagram's overflow, not leaving space > measure the comment box's right edge, and scale the page down (never below 75%) so there are always 20px between it and the panel
[extension] The 20px gap vanished again when the reader found no comments (my fit measured from read comments only) > measure from the "add a comment" box first, which is always there when comments are open
[extension] A still "Open comments" label is easy to miss > the label nudges 6px toward the icon it points at (1.8s ease-in-out loop), and stays still for people who turned on reduced motion
[extension] The lime "Open comments" label was hard to read over bright videos and competed with the lime circle > label is now black with white text; lime stays only on the circle, so one thing says "look here"
[extension] Instagram has two reel layouts. When comments sit beside the video from the start, my gap logic (built for the pop-up comment box) left a big empty strip next to the panel > detect the one rounded card that holds both the video and the comment box, and slide only that card so it is 20px from the panel. Sliding the whole page would cut the "more posts" grid below, so I move the card only
[extension] When new comments were read, the panel jumped back to the top while I was reading a card lower down > adding cards re-drew the list and scrolled to the focused card (card 1). New background cards now keep the panel's scroll position, and the bottom pill just says "N new cards added"
[extension] Got it / I knew this / Still confused could not be undone, and Still confused gave almost no feedback > every answered card shows one line with Undo ("✓ Added to Now you know", "✓ Hidden from now on", "✓ Marked confused"); the Now you know pill is now a button that opens the saved list, and its number jumps when it goes up so Got it and the count visibly connect
[UX writing] "Nothing to decode" and "Couldn't decode that one" are jargon: a person raised elsewhere may not know "decode" > plain words ("Nothing to explain", "Couldn't explain that one")
[extension] Read again re-read every comment from scratch, slow, with a loading line that was easy to miss > it keeps the answers we have and reads only comments that are new; no new comments says so right away
[extension] The Now you know list mixed things I learned (Got it) with things I already knew (I knew this), so "3 things you know now" was wrong > the list and the count are only Got it; I knew this has its own "Already knew" group so a wrong tap can still be removed
[scope] Comments in Korean, Arabic and other languages showed up under English posts > skip comments that are mostly another script (D27) and say how many were skipped in "What I read"; an explanation I can't check is worse than none
[extension] Explanations were English only > an EN | 한 switch in the "Explaining for" row (D19); the choice is remembered, switching re-explains the current post

[extension] "Already knew" cluttered the Now you know list but is the only way back from a wrong "I knew this" > a collapsed "Hidden for you (N)" shelf of small chips; the list rows link back to the original post
