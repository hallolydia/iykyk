IYKYK TEST SET
20 real comments. Text only. No usernames, no profile photos.

Mix
- didnt-get: 10  (I didn't get it)
- easy:       5  (I got it fully. Checks the AI doesn't underline for nothing)
- unclear:    3  (I half got it)
- sensitive:  2  (sad or personal. Checks the AI stays gentle)

How to fill each block
- group:     didnt-get / easy / unclear / sensitive
- source:    where it's from + one line about the scene (no usernames)
- comment:   paste the exact text
- my_gaps:   the exact phrases I didn't get, separated by " | "  (leave empty for easy ones)
- my_guess:  what I thought it meant before looking it up
- notes:     anything else (optional)

my_gaps and my_guess = the answer key. Fill them by hand. Not with AI.
