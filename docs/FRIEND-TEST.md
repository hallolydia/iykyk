# Friend test (10 minutes)

## 1. Install (3 steps, Chrome on a computer)
1. Unzip `iykyk-extension.zip`. You get a folder called `extension`.
2. Open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode** (top right), click **Load unpacked**, and pick the `extension` folder.
3. Pin the extension (puzzle icon > pin). Open any Instagram post or reel **with the comments open**, then click the iykyk icon.

(한국어) 압축을 풀고 → 크롬 주소창에 `chrome://extensions` → 오른쪽 위 **개발자 모드** 켜기 → **압축해제된 확장 프로그램을 로드합니다** → `extension` 폴더 선택 → 인스타 게시물에서 댓글을 열고 확장 프로그램 아이콘 클릭.

## 2. What to say to your friend (read this out, nothing more)
"Open some posts you normally scroll. Use it however you like. Say out loud what you think is happening. I won't help unless you're really stuck."

Do **not** explain the buttons first. What they get stuck on is the result.

## 3. Watch for these (write down yes / no)
| Moment | What you hope happens |
|---|---|
| Comments closed | They notice the lime circle on the comment icon and open the comments |
| First card | They know what "Got it" and "I knew this" do without asking |
| The mistake | They find **Undo** after tapping the wrong one |
| A missed phrase | They try selecting words and find **Ask IYKYK** on their own |
| After a while | They open the **Now you know** list |
| Language | They find the **EN | 한** switch (if they want Korean) |

## 4. Ask at the end (3 questions)
1. In your own words, what does this do?
2. Was there a card that was wrong, or one you didn't need? Which?
3. Would you leave it on while scrolling? Why or why not?

## 5. Save
Screenshots of the first screen and any confusing moment. One quote from them. These go into the README under "What a friend taught me".

## Good to know
- It reads only the comment text, never usernames or photos. What the person knows stays in their own browser.
- The live AI is shared: about 20 reads a minute per person. A few friends at once is fine.
