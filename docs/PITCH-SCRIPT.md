# Pitch script (about 2 min 45 s, roughly 380 words)

Record your screen with the iykyk panel open on Instagram. Speak at a calm pace. Numbers come from `eval/results.md`.
Do not show the sad comment (c03) on screen. Say the one line about it instead.

---

**[0:00] Hook. Screen: an Instagram post with the comments open, no panel yet.**

"I live in the US. I understand every word in these comments. I still don't get the joke.
It isn't the English. It's the history, the politics, the TV, and a sense of humor I didn't grow up with. And I can't ask a friend 'why is this funny?' every single time."

**[0:25] The product. Screen: open the panel, press Read.**

"So I built IYKYK. If you know, you know. Now you know.
It reads the comments and highlights only the phrases someone raised outside the US would likely miss. I don't have to ask."

**[0:45] One card. Screen: click a highlight, read the card.**

"One click opens a card that answers the question I would actually have: why is this funny, what's the reference, or is this rude or friendly. Short, like a friend texting. If it isn't sure, it says 'Needs checking' instead of guessing."

**[1:10] It remembers. Screen: Got it, then I knew this, then the Now you know list.**

"Got it saves it to my Now you know list. I knew this hides it on every future post, so the more I use it, the quieter it gets. And if I tap the wrong one, Undo."

**[1:30] What it missed. Screen: select words, press Ask IYKYK.**

"When it misses something, I just select the words and press Ask IYKYK. No typing, I never leave the page. And for people who read faster in Korean, one switch changes the explanation language."

**[1:55] Can you trust it? Screen: the results table in the README.**

"I can't just say an AI is accurate, so I built the test first. I labeled 27 comments by hand: the phrases I didn't get. Then I scored three versions of the prompt.
All three landed in the same place. About two thirds of the underlines were right, and it found 65% of my gaps. My target for underlines was 80%, so I'm not there yet, and I'm saying so.
What the test showed me: the misses are mostly plain vocabulary, like 'manchild' or 'bond investors', not culture. That's my next fix.
The code also checks that every highlight quotes the comment exactly, and a grief comment gets no joke."

**[2:25] Choices. Screen: decisions.md.**

"I made choices on purpose. It skips comments that aren't in English, because a wrong explanation is worse than none. I labeled the test set myself, because AI shouldn't grade AI. And I used AI to build the whole thing, and to find where it breaks."

**[2:45] Close. Screen: the logo.**

"IYKYK. Now you know."
